16 January | God needs us or do we need God

I just came back from Jummah prayer. Right after praying Jumma Namaz with jamat, i have a habit of Reading Surah Jumah and 10 aiyah of surah Kahf…

Frankly I am very interested in Surah Kahaf. I have a lot of thoughts and question around it, and someday i’d like to go deeper. Question like

  • Why is it sunnah to read this surah after Jummah namaz.
  • I understand why Surah Jumah: it’s named after the day, and it has clear instruction to come pray Jummah in jamat and then go on about the world finding all the blessings and bounty of Allah.
  • The three stories narrated in this surah (309 years in cave, Musa A.S. with Al Khidr, Dhul-Qarnain)
  • All three stories have ordinary non-prophet people experience the nature breaking it’s rules for them.
  • People of caving sleeping for 309 years,
  • Al Khidr being given knowledge the prophet of his time wasn’t given,
  • Dhul-Qarnain facing Yajoj Majoj.
  • Allah commands us (and Prophet Mohammed S.A.W.) to say insha allah after we say we will do something in the future, because when some jews came and asked about these three stories, prophet mohammed S.A.W. replied by saying to some effect “I’ll tell you later” but forgot to say Insha allah. So as a result allah delayed this story for 15 days.
  • We know Prophet is perfect in every aspect… so i’m confused thinking to my self: How can prophet forget to say Insha allah. Allah knows best.

How ever i would not like to talk about this topic in today’s essay (said he after writing 20 lines on it haha).

In my brain dump today i’d like to talk about why people worship.

Prophet Musa A.S. once said (Surah Ibrahim 14:8) that even if everyone on the planet were ungrateful to allah, then it won’t reduce Allah’s praiseworthy.

And inanother hadith (Sahih Muslim 2577), allah says that even if the enitire living being (humans + Jinns) would not reduce anything from Allah’s Kingdom.

So basically, Allah doesn’t need me to worship Allah. I need myself to worship Allah.

I feel like people pray allah not for allah’s sake and people defend Allah’s honor and name not for Allah’s sake. Sure it’s true that if you love someone, you defend their name if someone is misusing it.

Majority of muslims i know are born muslims. That is they were born in a muslim households. And in these household, like i was, we were taught about allah, islam and prophet. As we grew up listening to stories of prophets it slowly because part of our identity, and for some folks, the entire identity.

We are the way we are because we were lucky to be born in their religion.

“Lucky” because we were born into it.

The thing is, people worship allah for their ego. If someone says anything about Allah and they do not like it, their defence is not coming from the place of:

  • How can you speak something about Allah

But rather, from the place of

  • How can you say something about something I believe in.

So it’s not Allah they are defending, but rather their ego hidden in “something I believe in” and therefore the identity i build for myself in my head.

On surface level, the action looks similar. Raising voice or concern. But since both steps have different root cause, their future path look different too.

This is because one is defending an all knowing all seeing entity, and the other is defending a fragile ego.

Most people know this sub conscioulsly but would not want to bring it to their consciouls self, because if they do, they’ll have to let go of the ego.

I don’t have any more thoughts on this matter.

End of essay.
