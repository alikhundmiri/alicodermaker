Most of us don't miss Fajr on purpose.

The phone is on Silent. Sleep Focus is on. The adhan notification arrives, makes no sound, and sits on the Lock Screen until you wake up after sunrise. Then the whole day starts with that small, heavy feeling.

That was the problem my wife Sofiya and I kept running into, so we built [Waqt: Salah Habit Builder](https://waqt.alicodermaker.com/), an iPhone app for praying on time.

## What it does

- **A Fajr alarm that rings even on Silent.** Waqt's Premium alarms use Apple's AlarmKit (iOS 26), the same system the Clock app uses, so they ring through Silent mode and Sleep Focus.
- **A reminder at every prayer**, and a countdown to when each prayer's time ends, on the Lock Screen and in widgets.
- **A private log.** Mark each prayer as on time, qada (made up late) or missed, and watch the streak grow.
- **Fajr buddies.** Pair up with a friend or family member and nudge each other awake.

There's no account and no ads. Your prayer log stays on your phone.

## Why an app, and why us

There are already many prayer apps. Almost all of them send a notification at adhan time, and a notification is easy to sleep through. We wanted the one thing that matters most for Fajr: a sound loud enough to wake you, that Silent mode can't switch off.

Sofiya and I build it together, and we read every email. If you try it, tell us what's missing: [waqt.alicodermaker.com/support](https://waqt.alicodermaker.com/support/).
